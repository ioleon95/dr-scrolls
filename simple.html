🎴 Dungeon Rivals — the Verse System

The whole idea: players collect Verses that reshape their character — for better and worse.

🙂 Simple view 📖 Moderate 🛠️ Technical view 🧿 Scrolls
🃏

1. A Verse is a power-up with a catch

Every Verse gives you something good and something bad. The bigger the upside, the bigger the trade-off.

+2 Attack−2 Speed Immune to fire…but leaves a fire trail

Verses come in four grades — Standard, Doctrine, Sacred and Divine. Standard ones are plain, cheap and flexible; each rarer grade is more powerful, weirder and more flavorful — and eats far more of your power budget.

think: a power-up card that costs you a little something
▼
📖

2. A Scripture is the set of Verses you bring to battle

Your Character picks your God — that sets your theme, a bit like your team color. Then you build your Scripture under four simple rules:

at least 30 Verses a power budget of 60 or less no duplicates (singleton) at most one Divine Verse

The 60-Power budget is a ceiling, not a target, and you may pack at most one Divine Verse. Spend heavily on a powerful Verse and less power remains for the rest of your deck. The default is singleton; a specific God or Verse rule may allow an exception. There is no separate Sacred cap: the Power budget and minimum 30-card Scripture already constrain expensive cards.

think: your personal deck, themed around your God

Using your Verses

🛠️

Before the match

You build your Scripture — your deck of Verses — around your God’s theme.

think: packing your bag before a trip
⬆️

During the match

Each purchased level grants a draw to claim during Prep or Invade: reveal 3 Verses, select 1, discard 2 to Ether, subject to authored God/Verse exceptions. Closing keeps the same offer; select before claiming another draw. Effects apply immediately, legal choices remain available after Devotee death, and play never pauses.

think: level up, pick a reward
✨

…and special Verses can bend those rules

Some Verses and Gods change the numbers and words: keep more than one, get tossed Verses back, hold one for next time, or upgrade a slot into a stronger random Verse. That “rules can rewrite the rules” twist is what makes each God feel different.

think: the wild cards that break the normal rules
▼
⚙️

3. One calculator adds it all up

Your Verses and your God’s rules all feed the same calculator that decides your final stats. One place to add things up keeps the whole game fair.

think: the game’s scoreboard math
🦸🦹

4. Both sides buy levels, using different currencies

Start at level 1 with no free starting draw; stop at level 10. That means nine standard level-up draws, each normally offering 3 Verses to select 1.

🦸 Heroes earn Gold through Market trading and eligible Villain Minion kills, then buy levels 🦹 Villains earn Resonite from digging and killing Hero Minions or structures, then buy levels

Resonite is the Villain's Gold-equivalent currency: level purchases spend the same stash as other Resonite purchases. There is no separate Villain XP track; collecting currency does not automatically level you up.

The nine level-ups cost Heroes 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 22 Gold (126 total), and Villains 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50 Resonite (270 total). God milestones occur at levels 3, 6 and 9.

Approved RR rules, September 13, 2026. Reward amounts and unresolved God/card policies still need approval; this is design intent, not proof of implementation. See the Moderate view for the full table and purchase rules.

think: same ladder, different wallets
