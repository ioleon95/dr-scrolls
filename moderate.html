Before the match

🧩 Building a Scripture

A Scripture is the deck of Verses you bring into a match. Your Character determines your God, and your God sets your identity — a bit like how a commander sets your colors in Magic. You then build a legal Scripture:

  • 1
    Power ≤ 60. Every Verse has a Power Cost. Your whole Scripture must spend no more than 60 Power (budget cap, not a fixed target).
  • 2
    At least 30 Verses. More cheap Verses vs. fewer expensive ones is the core deckbuilding tension.
  • 3
    Singleton. No two copies of the same Verse — unless a God or a Verse rule specifically allows it.
  • 4
    Divine cap. At most one Divine Verse per Scripture. ❓ confirm cap for Sacred

The power system

Power Cost

Each Verse is priced. Stronger, higher-tier Verses cost more of your 60-Power budget.

The trade-off

Spend big on a few bombs, or spread thin across many reliable Verses. You can’t have both.

Downside scales

A Verse’s drawback grows with its power & tier — power is never free.

Verse filters — who may run a Verse

Every Verse carries a Faction, one or more Moral Traits, and may be God-Locked. Your God only accepts Verses whose traits it favors — that gate is what makes each God’s Scripture feel different.

Faction

Heroic Villainous Neutral

God-Locked

Optional. Exclusive to a single God — only that God’s devotees may run it.

Neutral Verses

Faction-neutral — legal in any Scripture, under any God.

🦹 Villain traits — the 7 Sins

Pride · Greed · Lust · Envy · Gluttony · Wrath · Sloth

🦸 Hero traits — the 7 Virtues

Humility · Charity · Chastity · Kindness · Temperance · Patience · Diligence

Classic pairings (sin ↔ virtue): Pride↔Humility · Greed↔Charity · Lust↔Chastity · Envy↔Kindness · Gluttony↔Temperance · Wrath↔Patience · Sloth↔Diligence.

How a God gates a Scripture. Each God favors a set of Moral Traits — and can mix sin & virtue. A devotee’s Scripture may hold Verses of those traits, plus that God’s God-Locked Verses, plus any Neutral Verse.
· e.g. The Skittering Maw favors Gluttony Greed Patience → Maw Scriptures accept those traits + Maw-exclusive + Neutral Verses. ❓ confirm each God’s trait set

Verse hierarchy

🃏 What a Verse is (and the tier ladder)

A Verse is a small, permanent effect with an upside and a scaling downside. Behavior is pure data — Verses only Add / Subtract / Multiply / Declare / Edit your stats through one shared calculator (see §Gods). Its Tier is authored, not earned. There is no rarity — the four tiers are the whole ladder.

Stat effects (+/- stats) are reserved for Standard & Doctrine. Sacred & Divine are text-first — their power lives almost entirely in their rules text, and they rarely (ideally never) carry raw +/- stats.

Standard · greyGeneric, flexible, cheap. Mild trade-off. Carries +/- stats.
+2 Attack −1 Speed
Doctrine · greenSharper stat effects, situational, mid cost.
+3 Attack −2 Defense
Sacred · purpleText-first. A keyword engine with a lighter scaling cost — rarely any stats.

Design bar: Sacred/Divine feel exotic and text-driven — you build a Scripture to counter-balance their scaling cost. Standard/Doctrine feel generic and stat-driven. Every Verse should feel fair — a genuine risk/reward choice.

The keyword engine

📜 Word Glossary

Verses are written as rules text. The words in quotes are parameters — Gods and Verses rewrite them (e.g. choose "one" becomes choose "three"). This is the language the whole draw runs on.

Zones — where Verses live

Scripture

Your built deck & draw pool — ≥30 Verses, ≤60 Power, singleton.

Slot

A limited space that holds one drawn/loaded Verse. 3 draw / 3 load.

Gospel

Your active zone — the Verses applied to your character. Max 6. When full you may replace one to add another; the replaced Verse goes to Ether.

Ether

Your discard zone. Discarded, Sacrificed, or replaced Verses go here — some keywords pull them back.

Core flow

Level up

The trigger. Each level-up runs one draw → activation.

Draw "N"

Reveal N Verses from your Scripture into your Slots.

Load

Placing a Verse into a Slot so it can resolve. Loading consumes a Slot.

May

Optional. may choose = you can decline.

Choose "N"

Keep N of the drawn Verses to activate.

Discard "N"

Send N Verses to Ether. Gone from your pool unless a rule returns them.

Activate

Resolve a Verse — apply its effect and place it in your Gospel (permanent for the match).

Next

The next Verse in Scripture order. Activate "Next" "one" = fire the top card.

from Scripture

Effect draws straight off your Scripture instead of a Slot (works even with all Slots locked).

Shipped keywords

Sanctify "N"

Return N Verses to the draw pool — they can appear again.

Reverent

Hold a Verse for your next level-up instead of resolving now.

Ascend

Target a Slot: discard its Verse, replace with a random stronger one, and lock the Slot (−1 future draw).

Sacrifice & Ether · the Saint

Sacrifice

Choose a Verse in your Gospel and discard it (→ Ether); then Gain benefit (e.g. Activate "two" this level-up, or Revive a Verse).

Revive

Choose a Verse from your Ether and Activate it — it returns to your Gospel.

Martyr

Triggered. When a Verse enters your Ether (discarded/Sacrificed), Gain benefit.

Gluttony & tempo

Devour "N"

Discard N loaded Verses; for each one, add a Devour Counter. Your Gain benefit scales with the counter. (Skittering Maw)

Surge

Play an additional Verse this level-up — cost: take a Negative effect (a debuff). (Endless Tempest)

Control & interaction

Smite

Destroy a random Verse from a target opponent's Gospel. (Tempest / Wrath)

Rebuke

Sanctify a Verse in a target player's Gospel — back to their draw pool. (Eternal Warden)

Seal

Make a Verse unremovable from a Gospel. (Eternal Warden)

Lament

Swap a Verse in your Gospel with a target player's.

Absolve

Unlock an Ascend-locked Slot — recover one draw. The counter to Ascend.

Timing & conditions

On "[event]"

Trigger binder — On Level, On Raid, On Structure-Fall, On Kill, On Relic. Ties Verses to the raid loop.

While "[condition]"

Continuous — active only while the condition holds.

Threshold "N"

Effect switches on once a counter/level reaches N.

Identity & build-rules

Zealot

Bonus while every Verse matches your God's Moral-Trait allow-list — rewards pure devotion.

Cut — revisit post-MVP (not in launch scope)

Desecrate (Q-O)

A target Verse loses its text — strip its power. Can hollow a Gospel Verse into an empty slot. Runtime Verse-mutation — deferred.

Synchronize (Q-O)

Combine two Verses in your Gospel into one. Runtime Verse-mutation — deferred.

Corrupt / Redeem (Q-P)

Flip a target's Moral Trait along a sin↔virtue pair (Villains Corrupt; Heroes Redeem). Runtime trait warfare + legality re-eval — deferred.

Relic (Ordain, Q-K)

A Verse that overrides singleton — you may run duplicates of it. Breaks absolute singleton — scoped out.

Gain benefit, "N", "[event]" are parameters Gods and Verses rewrite. Every keyword is authored as data (no bespoke per-Verse code) — see the engineering brief briefs/verse-keyword-engine.md.

Player structure · resolution

🔀 How a level-up resolves (Slots & the switch)

Every level-up flows through the same four beats. Loading needs Slots, and Ascend is how you trade Slots for power.

STEP 1Level up

You gain a level → a draw begins.

STEP 2Load

Verses fill your Slots (draw / discard / next).

STEP 3Interaction

You choose — if the rule gives you a choice.

STEP 4Activation

Kept Verse(s) resolve into your Gospel (max 6); discards fall to Ether.

Worked example. A rule says: Discard "two", then Activate "Next" "one" from Scripture. Here the Load step is the discard (2 Slots used), there’s no interaction, and activation comes off your Scripture.
· 2 Slots → discard 2, activate the next.
· Ascend one Slot → only 1 left → discard 1, activate the next.
· Ascend both → no discard → just activate from Scripture.

When every Slot is locked by Ascend you can no longer hand-load Verses (no selecting) — but effects that Activate Next or fire from Scripture still happen.

Gods · rules & identity

⛩️ God Rules and the Pantheons

Your Character locks in your God. Gods do two things mechanically: they rewrite the glossary words (their own rules text), and they add God Rules — modifiers that feed the same calculator your Verses use. God Rules can also change what your Scripture may contain (singleton overrides, allowed audiences). Gods sit above Verses in the hierarchy: God Rule > Verse rule > standard rule. ❓ confirm precedence order

🦸 Hero Pantheon — “they preach virtue, they harvest heroes”

The Radiant Judge — “Only obedience has value.”Individuality is sinful.
The Golden Shepherd — “All belongs to the faithful.”Generosity becomes confiscation & tithes.
The Eternal Warden — “Excess breeds corruption.”Security through control; every pleasure monitored.
The Sacred Banner — “Work is worship.”Unity through conformity; endless labor.
The Ivory Witness — “Mercy is correction.”Truth by authority; cruelty “for your own good.”
The Saint of Sacrifice — “Purity above desire.”Suffering is holy; suppresses love & freedom.
The Silent Martyr — “Endure. Question nothing.”Suffering celebrated instead of solved.

🦹 Villain Pantheon — “they preach freedom, they harvest villains”

The Skittering Maw — “The universe exists to be consumed.”Hunger creates greatness.
The Endless Tempest — “Anger is honesty.”Freedom through destruction.
The Queen of Excess — “Take what you can earn.”Ownership is proof of strength.
The Laughing Ruin — “Rest is the reward of freedom.”Refuse labor imposed by others.
The Horned Tyrant — “Demand the world acknowledge your worth.”Greatness never apologizes.
The Whisper Eye — “If another has it, surpass them.”Secrets are power; envy fuels ambition.

⚔️ Launch God Rules — the 4 playable Gods

The vertical slice ships 4 of the 14 Gods. Each adds one God Rule — a modifier that feeds the same calculator as your Verses (God Rule > Verse rule > standard). Shared cadence knob: “every 3 Levels.”

🦹 The Skittering Maw — Insatiable

+1 to ALL stats per Devour Counter you hold; you may Devour every 3 Levels. (Counters are permanent, so the reward is the scaling all-stat swell.)

🦹 The Endless Tempest — Overwhelm

Every 3 Levels you may Surge (Activate an extra Verse); you start with 2 Load zones instead of 3. (Tempo now, one fewer draw all match.)

🦸 The Eternal Warden — Equilibrium

All player stats are capped at 3× Level; every 3 Levels, Seal a target player’s Gospel Verse. (Anti-scaling control — the Maw’s foil.)

🦸 The Saint of Sacrifice — Martyrdom

When a Verse enters Ether, gain +1 to a stat; every 3 Levels Revive a Verse from Ether and Sanctify it. (Loss becomes fuel.)

Self-balancing rule (all Verses): a Verse’s drawback scales with its payoff and is tied to the same mechanic. Divine and Sacred Verses are text-first — they carry no raw +/- stats; their entire cost lives in the rules text as a scaling penalty. Divine Verses are the most powerful and the most punishing, so you build a Scripture to counter your own bombs. The intended feeling: “this makes me OP, but MAN I need to counteract the crazy negative.” Sacred carries a lighter scaling cost; Doctrine/Standard keep simple fixed +/- stat trade-offs.

Lore hook (the joke): both pantheons feed on the souls of champions. Heroes purge dungeons, Villains slay champions — both sides celebrate, both fatten the same gods. Each God accepts only Verses of its favored Moral Traits (plus Neutral and its own God-Locked Verses) — see Building a Scripture → Verse filters.

Progression

⬆️ EXP, Gold & what “Level” means

A Level is your mid-match growth tick: every level-up triggers a Verse draw. More levels = more Verses = a stronger character. Both sides climb the same ladder — they just pay for the rungs differently.

SideWhat you earnHow you earn itHow you level
🦸 Heroes Gold Keeping the peace & raiding the dungeon Buy your levels with Gold (the Market / Indulgence)
🦹 Villains XP Building dungeons & raiding Gain levels automatically as XP fills
