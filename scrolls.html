New Verse

Author a full Verse. Power must sit in the Tier band. Stat effects are for Standard & Doctrine only — Sacred & Divine are text-first (their power lives in the rules, rarely in +/- stats) and must carry a scaling downside (self-balancing pillar).

New God

A God is a Faction + a Moral-Trait allow-list + one God Rule (a modifier-Rule source). Devotees may inscribe Neutral Verses + this God's own Verses + any Verse matching a favored trait.

100 Verses · 36 Neutral core + 16 signature × 4 gods (Maw · Tempest · Warden · Saint). Filter by God to build one devotee’s deck — Neutral + that God’s Verses, within the 60 Power budget (min 30 Verses, singleton). Pair with the handbook for the keyword rules.
